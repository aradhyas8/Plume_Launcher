Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Plume Launcher ("Plume") is a home screen app for Android TV and Google TV, made by aradhyas8. This policy explains what Plume reads, what it sends, and to whom.

Summary

  • Plume has no account, no ads and no analytics.
  • Plume does not sell or share your data.
  • Plume has no server of its own. Your settings stay on your TV.
  • Plume sends a few requests to outside services, listed below, to show weather, wallpapers and, in the GitHub build, updates.

Data that stays on your TV

Plume reads the following data to show your home screen. It does not leave your TV.

  • Installed apps, to show the apps row and All apps.
  • TV listings and Continue Watching (Android TV's Watch Next list and app channels), to show what you were watching.
  • Photos, only if you choose a folder of your own photos for the wallpaper or screensaver.
  • TV inputs (HDMI sources), to show the Sources list.
  • Your settings, such as layout, theme, PIN and remote keys. They are stored in Plume's private storage on the TV.
  • Your approximate location, only if you turn on the weather or choose a city. Besides the weather, the Auto theme uses it to work out sunrise and sunset on the TV. That calculation sends nothing. Plume keeps the location on the TV after you turn the weather off, so the Auto theme still works. Clearing Plume's data in Android settings removes it.

Services Plume connects to

These services receive your TV's IP address with each request, as any internet request does. Plume sends no other personal data to them.

ServiceWhyWhen
ipwho.isFind your approximate city from your IP address, for the weatherOnly if weather is on and set to automatic location
Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com)Get the weather, and find a city you typeOnly if weather is on
Bing (www.bing.com)Download the daily wallpaper imageOnly if you choose the Bing wallpaper
GitHub (github.com, api.github.com)Check for and download updates in the GitHub build; download gallery images in either buildOnce a day for updates in the GitHub build; gallery only if you choose it

Each service has its own privacy policy. Plume does not control how they handle requests.

Adding photos from your phone

When you add a wallpaper by QR code, Plume runs a small web page on your TV for a short time. Your phone sends the photo straight to the TV over your home network. The photo does not go through the internet or any server.

Accessibility service

Plume includes an accessibility service. It is used for one purpose: to open Plume when you press the Home button, and to run the remote key changes you set up in Plume. The service listens to remote key presses and to which app's window is in front. It does not read screen content, text you type, or other apps' data. It does not store or send any of this information. You can turn the service off at any time in Plume's settings or in Android's Accessibility settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for the services above.
  • Read TV listings: for Continue Watching.
  • Read images: only to show photos you pick.
  • Install packages: only in the GitHub build, to install Plume updates when you ask. The Google Play build uses Play updates and does not request this permission.
  • Delete packages: to uninstall apps when you ask.

Children

Plume does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Storage and deletion

Plume keeps settings and photos added from your phone in its app storage on your TV. Android removes this app storage when you clear Plume's data or uninstall it. You can remove an added wallpaper in Plume settings. Photos in a folder you choose on the TV or a USB drive remain in that folder; Plume only reads them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
