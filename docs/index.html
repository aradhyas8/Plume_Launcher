Everything you use. Nothing you don't.

Glass you can see through

Tiles, cards and settings in Clear or Frosted glass, or solid. The selected tile rises out of its row and its edge catches the light. Text and icons change colour to stay readable on any wallpaper.

Wallpapers your way

250+ Apple, Amazon and NASA scenes, Bing’s photo of the day, your own photos or a USB drive. The Wallpaper theme picks light or dark from the picture.

Continue Watching

Resume shows and films from your apps, including Stremio and Nuvio, right from the home screen.

A clean apps row

Monochrome logos, colour logos, banners or an icon pack. Reorder, sort by most used, hide apps, or lock the layout with a PIN.

Your Home button

The Home button on your remote opens Plume, even on Google TV. Hold it for the Google TV dashboard.

Cue alerts

A small pill shows live scores for the teams you follow and warns you before rain, on Home and over your apps.

Remote keys and Sources

Remap the TV, Netflix or Settings button, with a separate action on hold. Switch HDMI inputs from the top bar.

No ads, no tracking

No sponsored rows, no account, no analytics. Your settings stay on your TV.

Other ways to install

USB drive. Download the APK, copy it to a USB drive and open it with a file manager on the TV.

adb. From a computer on the same network:

adb install Plume-1.8.apk
